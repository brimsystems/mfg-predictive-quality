Three things are watched between retrains: whether labels are complete enough to score against, whether each alarm layer's rate is drifting, and whether the per-shot measurement still sits within its gauge-error band. The retraining rule acts only on matured labels.

Label maturity

Most of a job's confirmed defects are known within a day of its last shot, because the sort review and the packing tallies close within the shift; returns arrive weeks to months later and are why a job is scored only after 21 days. 0 of 118 jobs are still provisional at the extract date.

Virtual metrology against each audit

Each audit piece is scored against its shot's prediction as it arrives. The retraining rule: retrain when the rolling error over the last 200 pieces of a mold and press stays above 1.5x gauge on weight or 2.0x on the dimension for 400 consecutive pieces, using only matured labels.
