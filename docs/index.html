Cavity Pressure Monitoring and Predictive Quality

An injection molder's instrumented cell: every shot retained, charted and measured, from the monitoring units to the quality dashboard.

Two presses, IM-11 and IM-12, run six molds with cavity pressure sensors. The platform joins the monitoring units' per-shot values to the MES, the resin lots, the setpoint log, the audits and the scrap tallies; runs the shop's control-chart rules on every shot; adds drift detection; and puts a predicted weight and dimension and an anomaly state on every shot.
